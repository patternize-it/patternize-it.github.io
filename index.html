The problem

An exam produces an impression. Rarely a number.

A finger-to-nose test, a scan of an EEG trace, a bedside cognitive check — each gives a real read on brain function in the moment, but almost none of it is recorded in a form you can compare to the last visit. Small changes get missed simply because nothing was measured the same way twice.

What we measure

If it's a signal of brain function, it belongs in the record.

Each of these runs today, inside the same patient record. The list is built to grow — every new signal is validated, then attached to the pattern you're already tracking.

Movement

An ordinary webcam turned into a measuring instrument for finger-to-nose and similar motor exams.

  • Trajectory & endpoint error
  • Tremor & smoothness
  • Left/right asymmetry

EEG

A recording rendered as topography and band power — scanned in a glance, not paged through trace by trace.

  • Frequency-band power
  • Hemispheric symmetry
  • Change vs. own baseline

Cognitive testing

Standardized tasks like Mini-Cog, administered without an instructor, in English or Persian.

  • Automatic clinical scoring
  • Recall, clock-drawing, timing
  • Response latency & pauses

Voice

  • Prosody & pacing

Approach

The neurologist stays the neurologist.

Patternize doesn't produce a diagnosis or a verdict. It gives a more exact version of what you already look for, and hands the interpretation straight back to you.

Every measurement is labeled with how it was obtained, so you can see what a number rests on before you trust it.

1

The patient performs a standardized assessment

The same task you'd already request — administered through the app, the same way every time.

2

The raw signal is captured alongside it

Camera, EEG, and interaction timing are recorded while the task runs, without changing how the patient performs it.

3

Signal becomes quantified measurement

Movement, spectral features, and the validated clinical score are extracted as separate, labeled layers.

4

You read the pattern

This visit sits next to the previous ones. What changed, where, and whether it matters is entirely your call.

Session data

Charted the moment it's captured.

Each domain gets its own line, plotted the moment a new session is recorded. A steady gain, a slow decline, or a dip and recovery shows up as a shape — not a column of numbers to compare by eye.
