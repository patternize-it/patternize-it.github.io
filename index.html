WHAT WE MEASURE

If it's a signal of brain function, it belongs in the record

Each of these runs today, inside the same patient record. The list is meant to grow — every new signal is added the same way the first ones were: validated, then attached to the pattern you're already tracking.

Camera — movement & expression

An ordinary webcam, turned into a measuring instrument: finger-to-nose and gait tracked in three dimensions, facial expression and asymmetry scored the same way, every visit.

  • Trajectory, tremor, endpoint error
  • Facial asymmetry & expression dynamics
  • Anything else the camera can resolve, added as it's validated

EEG — read visually

A recording rendered as a topographic map and band-power breakdown, so it's something you scan in a glance, not something you page through trace by trace.

  • Frequency-band power over time
  • Scalp topography & hemispheric symmetry
  • Change measured against the patient's own baseline

Cognitive testing

Standardized tasks like Mini-Cog, administered and scored automatically — the validated clinical score, plus the timing and hesitation behind it.

  • Guided, standardized administration
  • Automatic scoring against the clinical scale
  • Response latency, not just the final result

Voice — next in line

Speech carries its own markers of brain function. Voice is the next signal joining the record, followed by whatever else proves worth measuring.

The direction is a single record for anything measurable about brain function — camera and EEG and cognition today, voice next, and more as the science catches up.

APPROACH

The neurologist stays the neurologist.

Patternize doesn't produce a diagnosis or a verdict. It gives a more exact version of what you already look for, and hands the interpretation straight back to you.

Every measurement is labeled with how it was obtained, so you can see what a number rests on before you trust it.

1

The patient performs a standardized assessment

The same task you would already request — administered through the app, in the same way every time.

2

The raw signal is captured alongside it

Camera, EEG, and interaction timing are recorded while the task runs, without changing how the patient completes it.

3

Signal becomes quantified measurement

Movement, expression, spectral features, and the validated clinical score are extracted as separate, clearly labeled layers.

4

You read the pattern

This visit is placed next to the previous ones. What changed, where, and whether it matters remains entirely your call.

OVER TIME

Not a score. A trajectory.

A single visit tells you where a patient is today. Patternize is built around the visit after that — and the one after that.
