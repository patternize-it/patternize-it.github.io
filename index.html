Every exam leaves a pattern. Patternize is how you keep following it.

A standardized cognitive task, a finger-to-nose test, an EEG recording — each already tells you something. Patternize turns them into one quantified, patient-specific record you can compare visit over visit, without changing how you examine a patient.

Three families of signal, read the same way every time

A clinical exam already produces this information. Patternize doesn't replace the exam — it captures it more precisely, and keeps every reading attached to the patient it came from.

COGNITION

Assessments a patient can complete alone

Starting with Mini-Cog: three-word recall and a clock drawing, guided by the app and scored on the standard 0–5 scale — no instructor needs to be in the room.

  • Response timing and pauses
  • Drawing trajectory and corrections
  • Local speech recognition, including Persian prompts
MOVEMENT

The camera as a measuring instrument

Finger-to-nose and related motor tests, tracked through the webcam and turned into figures instead of an impression of "looks a little off."

  • Trajectory, velocity, and endpoint error
  • Tremor and movement smoothness
  • Left/right asymmetry across visits
NEURAL ACTIVITY

EEG, read at a glance

Spectral and spatial features shown as a readable topomap next to the rest of the panel — built to be scientifically meaningful without being a wall of traces.

  • Frequency-band activity over time
  • Spatial distribution and connectivity
  • Change against the patient's own baseline

The neurologist stays the neurologist.

Patternize doesn't score a diagnosis. It gives you a more exact version of what you already look for in an exam, and hands the interpretation back to you.

1

Patient performs a standardized assessment

Mini-Cog, finger-to-nose, an EEG recording — the same task you'd already ask for, administered through the app.

2

Patternize captures the raw signal

Camera, audio, EEG, and interaction timing are recorded alongside the task, without changing how the patient completes it.

3

Raw signal becomes quantified measurement

Timing, trajectory, spectral features, and the validated clinical score are extracted as separate, clearly labeled layers.

4

You interpret the pattern

The dashboard shows this visit next to the last one. What changed, and where, is always your read — Patternize just makes it visible.
